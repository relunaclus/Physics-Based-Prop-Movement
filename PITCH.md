# Puzzle Game Idea

## The basics

The basic idea of my game is a simple physics based first person 3D puzzle game where the player must move different objects in an environment in order to complete puzzles and complete the game.  

The puzzles will involve physics-affected props which the player can "pick up" and move by holding down an input, and put down wherever they need to.

These props can be used in a variety of different ways, such as activating buttons, acting as a prop for the player to jump onto and climb up, blocking liquids, ect. By reusing these props and their respective behaviours, the player will hopefully grow accustomed to how they work and be able to intuite how to solve the various puzzles across the game. The game will therefore expand on the extreme fundamentals of simple physics props that the player can move.

## Different Prop Types

The base prop will likely be a simple cube-shaped prop that is useful for a variety of things, but additional props with different shapes will be added that can slightly change how they interact with the environment. For example, just comparing a cube-shaped prop and a sphere-shaped prop can highlight an important difference, with the player being easily able to use the cube as a sort of stepping stool to jump on top of perhaps in order to access a higher up area, while the player would not be able to do the same with the sphere, but the sphere may roll in a way that the cube would not, allowing it to maybe roll down a slope and into an area that the cube would not.

## Targeted Platform and Hardware

The game would most likely be ideal on PC with a keyboard and mouse, though realistically due to its simplistic nature and controls, it would likely still work well enough with a controller or on a console. I also think that the game might work well as a VR game as it has a similar "pick-up props by pointing at them" functionality to already existing VR games such as Job Simulator.
